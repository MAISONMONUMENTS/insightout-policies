Privacy Policy — Insight Out

13 November 2026

Insight Out uses the authorized account's basic profile information, the owner's selected video, and its caption and hashtags. It does not read or collect other people's content or personal information.

Working data is stored on the owner's computer only. No tracking, analytics, or advertising services are used.

Nothing is sent for posting until the owner approves the individual post. The approved video and caption go to TikTok for publication under TikTok's privacy policy and terms.

The owner may stop using the tool at any time, delete local data, or revoke access from the platform's settings.

Questions: contact the account owner through the usual contact channel.
