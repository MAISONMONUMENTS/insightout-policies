Terms of Service — Insight Out

13 November 2026

Insight Out is a creator tool for small businesses. Each business connects its own Instagram and TikTok accounts and posts its own content. Insight Out, a soccer-training brand, is the first business using it.

The business owner retains ownership of all videos and is responsible for the rights to that content and for complying with each platform's rules. Nothing is posted until the owner selects a video and approves that individual post. Only the owner's own content is ever used; the tool does not collect other people's content or personal information.

Working data is stored on the owner's computer. For an approved post, the selected video and caption are sent to TikTok for publication under TikTok's terms and privacy policy.

The owner may stop using the tool at any time, delete local data, or revoke TikTok access from the platform's settings.

Questions: contact the account owner through the usual contact channel.
